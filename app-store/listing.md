## Uygulama gizliliği (App Privacy)

"Do you or your third-party partners collect data from this app?" sorusuna **No** cevabını ver. Mağazada **Data Not Collected / Veri Toplanmıyor** etiketi çıkar. Oyun ayarları sadece cihazda saklanıyor, hiçbir yere gönderilmiyor; bu Apple'ın tanımına göre veri toplamak sayılmaz.

**Açıklama / Description** (1156/4000)
```
All Green is a secret number duel for two. You'll learn the rules in a minute; mastering them takes a little longer.

HOW TO PLAY
Each player picks a secret 4- or 5-digit number. Then you take turns guessing your opponent's number. Every digit of a guess gets a color:
• Green: right digit, right spot
• Yellow: the digit is in the number, but in a different spot
• Red: the digit is not in the number
The first to turn every tile green wins.

TWO MODES
• Two players: Take turns on one phone. A pass-the-phone screen appears while secrets are entered, so nobody sees the other's number.
• Vs computer: Easy, Medium and Hard levels. The computer never peeks at your number; it plays using the same color clues you see.

YOUR RULES
• 4- or 5-digit numbers
• Repeated digits (like 1123) on or off
• Equal turns: if the first player cracks it, the opponent gets one last try

EASY TO PICK UP
• Big, colorful keypad. Digits you've tried get colored on the keys too.
• Score is kept across rematches.
• Light and dark theme
• English and Turkish

NO ADS, NO DATA
No accounts, ads, tracking or in-app purchases. No internet needed. No personal data is collected.
```
